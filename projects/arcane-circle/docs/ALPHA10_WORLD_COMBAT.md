# Alpha.10 world combat contract

- 100 visible player vitality equals vanilla 20 health.
- Every non-player living entity receives the same 5x pool and 5x incoming-damage conversion.
- Mage and calamity multipliers apply after this neutral baseline.
- Quest difficulty is fixed from 견습 through 재앙 and does not read player/issuer circle.
- Named faction representatives and calamity bosses use permanent-death saved data.
- Late-game regions are deterministic, visible world regions rather than a save-breaking biome registry replacement.
