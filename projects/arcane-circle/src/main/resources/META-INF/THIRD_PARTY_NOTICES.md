
# Arcane Circle Third-Party Notices

## Dungeons & Dragons System Reference Document 5.1 / 5.2

Spell names and high-level mechanical archetypes are adapted from the Dungeons & Dragons System Reference Document,
made available by Wizards of the Coast LLC under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
This mod changes numerical balance, presentation, progression and Minecraft implementation. Wizards of the Coast does
not endorse this project. No proprietary setting characters, locations, deities, logos, monsters, maps or artwork are included.

## Ceremonial sigil references

The geometric visual language of nested rings, polygons, cardinal lines and seals was informed by public-domain
ceremonial magic manuscripts including Francis Barrett's *The Magus* (1801) and public-domain grimoire collections.
No scanned plate is redistributed; the in-game particles are newly rendered geometry.

## Academy architecture

The academy is generated from vanilla blocks using an original layout informed by public-domain Gothic collegiate
architecture. No third-party Minecraft schematic or proprietary fantasy-school map is bundled.
