# TITANBREAK first playable slice

The first playable milestone is a complete low-tier hunt/augmentation loop rather than a content-complete game.

## Included loop
- Five normal hunt species: Ripper, Skitter, Bulwark, Needler, Howler.
- Two elite species: Chrono Hound and Null Eye.
- One multipart boss: The Pursuer.
- Hunt material drops, first-discovery Research Data, Adaptation XP/levels/points.
- Fabricator I assembly and dedicated augmentation fabrication screen.
- Surgical Bay assembly and persistent install/remove flow.
- Twelve Fabricator-I augmentation families with real slots; core combat/mobility/analysis effects are wired for this slice.
- Analysis, threat-warning, hook and Reflex Drive controls.
- Server-authoritative encounter pacing that preferentially exposes undiscovered normal species, then elites, then The Pursuer after actual augmentation progression.
- Custom health/sanity/progression HUD remains the player-facing combat HUD.

## Pursuer gate
The first Pursuer encounter is exposed after all five normal species and both elite species have been recorded, at least two augmentations are installed, and Adaptation Level 4 is reached. This intentionally requires the player to use the hunt -> fabricate -> surgery loop before the boss arrives.

## Not part of this milestone
This milestone does not claim the final content-bible counts. Higher tiers, the rest of the 48 augmentation families, later bosses, world structures, final bespoke models/animations/audio, full terrain breach, and the final Temporal Rating table remain later production work.

## Validation target
The milestone is accepted when a fresh singleplayer world can progress from ordinary hunting through facility construction and augmentation installation to a Pursuer kill without creative-only state manipulation, while the existing Reflex Drive and multipart regressions remain clean.
