# Living Kingdoms Revision 4 Playtest

## Controls

- `M`: open the authored-world atlas.
- `K`: open the detailed character status record.
- Inventory: use the `지도 [M]` and `상태 [K]` buttons at the top-left.

## Existing-save migration

Revision four preserves the origin profile, loadout, NPC-death records and crime records. On first login it clears only known early-playtest construction envelopes, rebuilds the selected homeland, moves the player to the saved residence and marks that homeland as revision 4.

## Required checks

- No detached roof rows, oversized floating logs or prototype market canopy remain.
- Erden has a connected palisade and supported market roofs.
- Houses and remote starts have clear two-block headroom and blended terrain approaches.
- The map shows the three starter realms, current position, residence and trade roads.
- The status record shows real server values for identity, vitals, equipment and wanted state.

## Deferred after the essential repair

- Infinite noise-based fantasy continent generation outside the authored starter regions.
- Custom biome blocks and CC0 texture integration.
- Regional monsters, wildlife and tools.
- Profession workstations and non-vanilla advanced crafting.
